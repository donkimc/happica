1. Starting and stopping

Happica runs four local services: two copies of ComfyUI (one for images and video, one for music), the backend (the job queue), and this web app. One script starts them all:

cd ~/Project/happica
scripts/start.sh

Then open http://127.0.0.1:5173. Press Ctrl+C in that terminal to stop everything.

For story mode and narration, also open the Ollama app (it runs in the menu bar).

The status pill in the top-right corner tells you whether everything is ready:
  • Ready · mps — everything is running, on your Mac's GPU.
  • Ready · mps · story mode off — Ollama isn't running; line mode still works.
  • ComfyUI offline — the model server is still starting (give it ~20 seconds) or has stopped.
  • Backend offline — scripts/start.sh isn't running.

2. Your first video

  1. Click the Narrated story preset at the top of the form.
  2. Type a short story — three or four sentences is plenty. For example: “A curious fox cub leaves its den on the first snowy morning. It chases snowflakes through a birch forest. At night it follows the northern lights home.”
  3. Pick a voice if you like, and check the time estimate next to Generate.
  4. Click Generate. The job appears below the form with a live progress bar showing each stage: scenes → narration → images → video → assemble.
  5. When it says Done, the video plays right in the page. Right-click it (or use the player's menu) to download.

You can keep using the app while a job runs. Jobs run one at a time — the GPU only fits one — so new jobs wait in a queue.

4. Scene modes

One line per scene

Each line you type becomes one scene, and the line is used directly as the image description. Use this when you know exactly what each shot should show. If narration is on, the line is also what gets spoken.

Story — AI writes the scenes

Write in plain prose. A local language model (Ollama) splits it into scenes and writes three things for each: a detailed image prompt, a motion hint for AI motion, and a line of narration. You'll see the narration in quotes under each scene once the job runs.

6. Working with results

Click any video in History to open it above the gallery. From there:

ActionWhat happens
Regenerate (under a scene)Redraws just that scene with a new random seed. You can edit its image prompt first — useful when a character looks different from the other scenes. The narration and timing stay the same, and the final video is rebuilt.
Re-runMakes a new video from the same text and settings with new seeds — different images.
Re-run, same seedReproduces the same images exactly. Handy after changing nothing but, say, the voice.
Edit & re-runLoads the text and settings back into the form so you can tweak them and generate again.
Change soundSwap the background music and ambience (Off, Auto, or from your libraries) or change their levels. Only the soundtrack is rebuilt — a few seconds for library picks or level changes, 1–2 minutes for a new Auto score. The lines under the video show what is playing.
DeleteRemoves the job and all its files.
CancelStops a queued or running job.

Click a scene's image to open it full size. The Subtitles (.srt) link under the scenes downloads the captions.

7. Tips for better videos

  • Keep characters consistent. Each image is generated separately, so describe recurring characters the same way every time (“a red fox cub with a white chest and black paws”). If one scene drifts, use Regenerate with the corrected description.
  • Be concrete. “A wooden rowboat on a misty lake at dawn, soft golden light” beats “a peaceful morning”.
  • Short narration reads better. One or two sentences per scene, around 10–25 words.
  • Preview first. Try an idea with Quick preview or Narrated story, then switch to AI motion once the images look right — use Re-run, same seed to keep them.
  • Better storyboards: start with OLLAMA_MODEL=qwen2.5:7b scripts/start.sh. The larger model keeps characters more consistent than the default 3B one.

8. How long things take

Measured on an Apple M2 with 16 GB of memory:

StepTime
Story → scenes (AI)~15 s
Narration~5–10 s per scene
Images~25 s to load the model, then ~5 s per image
Pan & zoomunder 1 s per scene
AI motion (LTX-Video)~6.5 min per 3-second scene

AI motion uses most of your Mac's memory. Close other heavy apps while it runs, and expect the fans to spin up.

9. Music Studio

Open the Music tab at the top to make songs — with sung lyrics or instrumental — using ACE-Step 1.5, a local music model. Every song is made in two takes so you can pick the better one.

Simple — describe it

Describe the song in plain words, in any language: “an upbeat indie pop song about riding a bicycle through the city on the first warm day of spring”. Pick a length (and Instrumental if you want no vocals), then:

  • Write song — the local AI writes a title, style tags, lyrics sized to the length, and picks the BPM, key and language (about 15–25 s). It opens them in Custom mode so you can change anything before creating.
  • Write & create — same, but goes straight to making the two takes.

Songwriting uses qwen2.5:7b in Ollama (falls back to the story model if it isn't installed). The larger model keeps lyrics in your language and writes shorter, singable lines.

Custom — write it yourself

  1. Style: describe the genre, mood, instruments and voice — e.g. synthwave, energetic, warm synth pads, female vocals. Click the + chips to add common tags.
  2. Lyrics: write them in sections. Click [verse], [chorus], [bridge]… to insert a section tag where your cursor is. Or switch on Instrumental to skip lyrics.
  3. Pick the length (30 s – 4 min), BPM, key and time signature. For vocals, set the lyrics language (51 languages).
  4. Click Create song. The library card shows progress: writing the song (the model plans melody and structure), then rendering audio.
  5. Play each take with ▶, or click anywhere on the waveform to jump there. MP3 downloads the take.
Song lengthTime for 2 takes (M2, 16 GB)
30 s~2 min
1 min~3 min
2 min~5–6 min
  • Lyrics tips: short lines sing better than long ones. Repeat the chorus — songs need a hook. A 30-second song fits about one verse and one chorus.
  • Songs are mastered to streaming loudness (−14 LUFS), trimmed of trailing silence and faded out at the end.
  • Re-run makes two new takes with the same settings; Edit & re-run loads them into the form.

Songs you make here appear in the video form under Background music → From Music library.

10. Sound effects

The Sound FX tab makes effects and ambiences from a description — sea waves, a summer night, a Buddhist temple, falling leaves, rain on a window, a door creaking — using Stable Audio 3, a local model made for sound effects. Click a quick idea to fill in a description, or write your own.

Length3–45 seconds.
Seamless loopBlends the end into the start so the sound can repeat forever without a click or jump. Use it for ambiences (waves, rain, crickets); leave it off for one-shots (a door, footsteps, a whoosh). Loops repeat when you preview them.
AI adds detailThe local AI turns a short idea (“night sound”) into a detailed sound description before generating. The card shows the description it used.
TakesTwo variations to choose from, or one.

Ambience in videos

In the Video tab, Ambience adds an environment bed under the whole video, alongside the music: Auto describes the setting from your story and makes a seamless loop (about 30 s extra), or pick any sound from your Sound library. Like the music, it dips while the narrator speaks. Finished videos can swap both with Change sound; New score / New ambience make a fresh Auto version.

11. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Generate is greyed out in story modeOllama isn't running, or the model isn't installed. Open the Ollama app; if the message says the model is missing, run ollama pull qwen2.5:3b.
A job failedIts error appears in red on the job. Images made before the failure are kept. Re-run it, or check outputs/logs/comfyui.log and outputs/logs/backend.log.
“ComfyUI offline” stays onRestart with Ctrl+C and scripts/start.sh. Look at the end of outputs/logs/comfyui.log for the reason.
AI motion is much slower than ~7 min a sceneThe Mac is short on memory and swapping. Close other apps (browsers with many tabs, other AI tools) and try again.
Narration sounds wrong or garbledThe voice and the text are in different languages. Pick a voice that matches the story's language.
“The music engine isn't running”The music copy of ComfyUI (port 8189) is down. Restart with scripts/start.sh; its log is outputs/logs/comfyui-music.log.
A character changes between scenesSee “Keep characters consistent” above — Regenerate the odd scene with a precise description.

Everything is stored locally inside the project folder:

outputs/logs/Logs for troubleshooting
models/The AI models (~31 GB)
